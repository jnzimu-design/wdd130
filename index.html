Kinshasa

Kinshasa is the capital city of the Democratic Republic of the Congo and one of the largest cities in Africa. Located along the Congo River, it is the country’s political, economic, and cultural heart. Known for its vibrant music, diverse communities, and rich history, Kinshasa is a dynamic city that reflects the energy and creativity of the Congolese people.
